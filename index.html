About Tasteful

How we build

On-device by default

Your recipes, lists, and plans live on your phone and sync through your own iCloud account. There’s no Latchkey database with your name on it, because there’s no account system at all.

Nothing to sign up for

No email capture, no onboarding funnel, no advertising SDKs, no analytics following you between apps. You download it and it works.

Honest pricing

The AI-assisted features cost real money to run, so they’re an optional subscription. Everything else in the app is included permanently, with nothing held back to force an upgrade.

About the studio

Latchkey Software LLC is a small independent software company registered in North Carolina. We’d rather build a short list of apps and maintain them properly than a long list we can’t keep up with.

The name is the latchkey ethic: let yourself in, figure it out, and make something good with the time you’ve got.
