What it does

Save from any link

Paste a recipe URL and Tasteful extracts the ingredients, steps, timings, and nutrition — including from sites that don’t publish clean structured data.

Import from a photo

Photograph a cookbook page, an index card, or a screenshot, and Tasteful reads the recipe right off it.

Cook Mode

Step-by-step cooking with the screen awake. Ask a question mid-step — “what can I use instead of buttermilk?” — and pick up exactly where you left off.

Your pantry, tracked

Keep track of what you have and when it expires, then ask what you can make from it tonight.

A grocery list that fills itself

Build the list from the recipes you’ve planned, or scan a handwritten list or a receipt to add everything at once. Ask substitution questions while you’re standing in the aisle.

Meal planning without the spreadsheet

Lay out the week across breakfast, lunch, and dinner, and ask for variety suggestions drawn from your own saved recipes.

Search the way you’d say it

“Something quick with chicken and no dairy.” Tasteful searches your collection by meaning, not just by keyword.

Nutrition, honestly labelled

Real nutrition data when the source publishes it, careful estimates when it doesn’t — and the estimates are always marked as estimates.

Yours, on your device

No account, no sign-up. Your collection lives on your phone and syncs privately through your own iCloud.

Try the beta
